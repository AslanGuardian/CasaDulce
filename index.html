La Casa

Pick a seat. Come back for the others.

One house, three moods. By day the light pours in over cane chairs and blue zellige. By night the candles come out and the bar takes over. Every corner is built for staying a little longer than you planned.

Swipe the house

The Bar

A river-stone terrazzo bar, glassware overhead and agave on the shelves. Pull up a stool and watch your espresso martini come together.

Best fora first date, flying solo, watching the pour

The Lounge

Cane chairs, café tables, a hand-tufted mural and a terracotta mosaic banquette under blue zellige. Ask for the banquette.

Best fordate night, catching up, dessert for two

The Terrace

Picnic tables under string lights, with the STRAT glowing over your shoulder. The best seat in the house when the sun goes down.

Best forgroups, happy hour, sunsets

Take the whole house.

Birthdays, launches, the dinner after the dinner. Book the bar, the lounge and the terrace for your people, with our bar team pouring for you.
