The House

Intimacy is
the new luxury.

Sunlight on plaster.
Something sweet in hand.

By day, Casa Dulce is the bright corner of The Distrikt for craft coffee, matcha and Latin-inspired ice cream — the kind of place you stop for one latte and stay for the afternoon.

Dinner is a reservation. What comes after is a scene. Casa Dulce is where the night migrates once the plates are cleared — a candlelit room built for the last, best hours of the evening.

Everything here is sourced like it matters, because it does. Our ice cream flavors were developed with top chefs in Mexico and are produced exclusively for this house — all natural, imported directly, available nowhere else. The pastry program runs out of our own ovens. The bar builds every cocktail to order, one perfect pour at a time.

Terrazzo, oak and cane, hand-troweled plaster, low amber light. Dress well. Stay late.

Cane chairs, hand-troweled plaster, blue zellige and a mural made by hand. Bring a friend. Stay for happy hour.

Arrive sweet. Leave sweeter.

The Signature · by day

The Matcha Daddy Martini.

For the matcha lovers. Our matcha, shaken into a martini and poured up — the greenest way to start happy hour, and the one everyone photographs first.

Not drinking? Try the Green Monkey: matcha over our homemade cinnamon-banana syrup, finished with fried banana cream. Add a scoop of churro or fried banana ice cream and the afternoon is handled.

Green, cold, poured up.

The Signature

The Fried Banana Martini.

The drink people will fly in for. Bananas fried in-house and reduced with cinnamon into a syrup, layered over aged Nicaraguan dark rum and a touch of milk. Crowned with fried banana and a cinnamon cold foam, dusted table-side.

Then the move: a giant sweet plantain chip laid across the rim — your spoon and your first bite in one. Scoop the foam. Eat the chip. Drink the martini. Order another.

One drink, three textures, zero shortcuts.

The Room

Designed to be photographed.
Built to be lived in.

Every surface was commissioned like a piece of jewelry. A bar faced in river-stone terrazzo. Cane chairs and candlelit café tables. A hand-tufted mural on hand-troweled plaster, and a banquette wrapped in terracotta mosaic under blue zellige. This is the backdrop your night deserves.

The Terrace · Private EventsThe Patio · Private Events

String lights, night air, the tower glowing.

Sun on the patio, an iced latte in hand.

Afternoons at The Distrikt run on cold foam and sunshine — Green Monkeys, Mexican mochas and café de olla made with our housemade syrups, out on the patio.

The terrace is where the night opens up — cocktails under café lights, desert air, the STRAT lit up over your shoulder. A skyline view that feels like a secret, because for now it still is.

For the nights that matter, take the whole house. Product launches, birthdays that deserve better than bottle service, the dinner-after-the-dinner. The room, the terrace, the full bar and pastry program — yours.
